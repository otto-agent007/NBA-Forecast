# Official season calendar capture

2026–27 full-feed capture: 1206 regular-season entries, including TBD placeholders.
This is the published season calendar, not a claim that all 1,230 assignments
are available. See calendar coverage and games_by_team.

`source.json.gz` preserves exact downloaded NBA bytes (decompress before checking
the provenance SHA256). `calendar.json.gz` is the normalized version with UTC,
venue-local times and revision metadata. Refresh using `fetch-schedule`; the
schedule can change. Preseason and playoff games are excluded.
